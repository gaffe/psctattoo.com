Tony started tattooing in New York City in the 1950s. His shop on 48th Street closed in 1961 because of the city's tattoo ban, which lasted until 1997. He then moved to Montreal and opened Point St-Charles Tattoo in 1976. In 1990, Dave Cummings began hanging around the shop, hoping to convince Tony to teach him to tattoo. His persistence paid off, and he started tattooing at the shop in 1993. In its 50th year, the shop has moved to Ville Émard, where its next 50 years begin.
